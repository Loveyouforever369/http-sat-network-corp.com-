A ready-to-render narration for an AI-avatar / voiceover explainer of the Capital Bridge design
toolkit. The script is **original**, written to pair with the visuals in `studio.html` and
`graphics-showcase.html`.

## Script A — 35-second Studio intro (hero / explainer)

> Meet the Capital Bridge Graphic Design Studio — the no-code way to create stunning, on-brand 3D
> graphics in seconds. Type your headline. Pick a brand color. Drag a slider to add depth, and watch
> your card come alive. When it's ready, copy the code or download it, and it's live on your site.
> From debt relief to investment intelligence, every card looks polished, professional, and
> unmistakably Capital Bridge. Design like a pro — no experience required.

## Script B — Course walkthrough (one line per beat, ~60s)

1. *Good design isn't talent — it's a few rules applied consistently. Let's build one card together.*
2. *Start with the system: our colors, our two fonts, spacing in steps of eight.*
3. *Lead with one clear headline. Keep the description short and easy to read.*
4. *Pick a brand color — every option already looks right, because it's from the system.*
5. *Add depth with a tilt and a little pop, so the icon lifts toward the viewer.*
6. *Hover to feel the light track your cursor. That's what makes it read as 3D.*
7. *Happy with it? Copy the code or download the file — and it's ready for the site.*
8. *That's the whole loop. Open the Studio and make your first card now.*

## Production notes

- **Voice:** warm, confident, professional. Pace ~150 wpm. Light, not salesy.
- **Visuals:** the animated 3D cards from `graphics-showcase.html` / `studio.html`; show the
  type → color → depth → copy steps.
- **Palette/type:** deep emerald `#094b3a` + emerald `#0d7a5f` + teal `#1a9d8f`, coral/gold accents,
  cream background; Fraunces headlines, Plus Jakarta Sans body.
- **Captions:** on-brand, high-contrast, keep lines short.
- **Length:** Script A ≈ 35s (hero); Script B ≈ 60s (walkthrough). Combine for a ~90s overview.
