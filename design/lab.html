The future is designed
by everyone.

A motivational note on how AI tore down the wall around great design — and handed you the brush. This is the script our AI avatar narrates.

Reads aloud in your browser. The rendered avatar version lives in narration-script.md + story.md.

Not long ago, great design lived behind a wall — years of training, expensive software, and a secret language of grids, kerning, and color theory. If you weren't "a designer," the door was closed.

That wall is gone.

Today, the tools meet you where you are. You bring the idea; the machine handles the craft you haven't learned yet. Pick a color, drag a slider, and depth and light appear — the way a studio would build it, in seconds, not weeks.

AI didn't replace the designer. It replaced the barrier. The blank page that used to intimidate now answers back. Skills that once took a decade are a starting point now, not a gate.

And here's the part that matters: taste still wins. The eye for what's clean, what's clear, what makes someone stop and feel something — that's still yours. The machine hands everyone a brush. What you paint is up to you.

So this is the invitation. Not to become a designer someday — to design today. Open the Studio. Make one card. Make it beautiful. Then make it better.

The future of design isn't a profession. It's a superpower — and it's in your hands now.
