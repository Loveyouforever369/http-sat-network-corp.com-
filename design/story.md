# "The Future Is Designed By Everyone" — Avatar Narration Story

An original, motivational story for the AI avatar to narrate — about the future of graphic design
and how AI removed the barrier to entry. ~90 seconds. Plays in the Design Lab's **Story** tab
(with a browser "Narrate" button) and is the script for the HeyGen / Invideo avatar render.

> Render with a **stock/licensed** avatar + voice (or your own). Original script — safe to voice anywhere.

---

## Full narration

Not long ago, great design lived behind a wall — years of training, expensive software, and a secret
language of grids, kerning, and color theory. If you weren't "a designer," the door was closed.

That wall is gone.

Today, the tools meet you where you are. You bring the idea; the machine handles the craft you
haven't learned yet. Pick a color, drag a slider, and depth and light appear — the way a studio
would build it, in seconds, not weeks.

AI didn't replace the designer. It replaced the *barrier*. The blank page that used to intimidate
now answers back. Skills that once took a decade are a starting point now, not a gate.

And here's the part that matters: taste still wins. The eye for what's clean, what's clear, what
makes someone stop and feel something — that's still yours. The machine hands everyone a brush. What
you paint is up to you.

So this is the invitation. Not to become a designer *someday* — to design *today*. Open the Studio.
Make one card. Make it beautiful. Then make it better.

The future of design isn't a profession. It's a superpower — and it's in your hands now. Welcome to
the Capital Bridge Design Lab. Let's build something the world has to notice.
