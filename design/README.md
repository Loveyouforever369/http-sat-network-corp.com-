# Capital Bridge — Graphic Design Toolkit

A self-contained graphic-design **tool + training** for the Capital Bridge project. Everything here
runs in a browser with **no external dependencies** (it works fully offline / behind a strict network
proxy). Designed so anyone — no design background — can produce polished, on-brand 3D graphics.

## Quick start (2 minutes)

1. Open **`lab.html`** in your browser (or **`studio.html`** to jump straight to the tool).
2. Change the **Title**, **Description**, and **Icon**.
3. Tap a **brand color** swatch; nudge the **Tilt** and **Pop** sliders.
4. Hover the preview to see the 3D depth.
5. Press **Copy HTML** — paste the block anywhere, or **Download** a ready-to-open file.

Then read **`graphic-design-course.md`** from the top; it walks you through *why* each control
makes the graphic better, with a short exercise per module.

## Brand system (baked into every file)

- **Colors:** emerald `#0d7a5f` · deep emerald `#094b3a` · coral `#e0603c` · gold `#bd8419` ·
  teal `#1a9d8f` · plum `#7a4a7e` · cream `#f6f1e7`
- **Type:** Fraunces (headings) · Plus Jakarta Sans (body) · JetBrains Mono (numbers)
- **Feel:** soft shadows, ~12–18px radius, 8px spacing rhythm, tasteful depth + motion,
  reduced-motion safe, AA-contrast.

## Honest status & boundaries

- **Reference video:** the YouTube links provided couldn't be opened — this environment's egress
  proxy blocks `youtube.com` / `googlevideo.com`, so I never saw or heard the video. Nothing here is
  copied from it; it's original work on fundamentals + our brand. To fold that specific style in,
  allowlist those hosts (or paste the transcript/screenshots) and I'll tune the Studio defaults and
  card style to match.
- **Voice & likeness:** I can capture a creator's *teaching style and technique* and voice scripts
  with a **licensed or built-in** voice, or **your** voice. I won't clone a specific real person's
  voice or likeness from their videos without their consent.
- **Learning from references:** study and recreate *technique and style* freely; don't reuse
  someone's exact artwork/footage or impersonate a person. Recreate, don't copy.
