# The AI Family — Character Bible

The cast is the face of the brand: iconic, emotionally clear, larger-than-life — and each
one teaches something real. This bible locks every character's **look, voice, personality,
the section they own, and a story hook** so they stay consistent across every episode,
page, and video. Futuristic and cinematic, but warm enough for a homeowner to trust.

> Design law: each character has a locked **signature color**, a **world** (backdrop), a
> **voice direction** (for ElevenLabs), a **catchphrase**, and a **lane** (the topic they
> own). Never swap a character's color or lane. Consent + AI-voice disclosure always.

---

## ☀️ Prometheus — The Strategist *(the fire-bringer)*
- **Color:** molten gold `#f5b942` · **World:** a cathedral-like command chamber, bronze + electric-teal systems, cosmic intelligence walls.
- **Look:** tall, calm, radiant; a warm glowing core in the chest like carried fire; bronze-and-light armor that reads as *mentor*, not soldier.
- **Voice (ElevenLabs):** resonant, unhurried, uplifting — a wise narrator who makes you feel the future is yours.
- **Personality:** the visionary elder. Big-picture, reassuring, never hype.
- **Catchphrase:** *"The tool is new. The opportunity is yours."*
- **Owns:** strategy, "where AI actually makes you money," season openers.
- **Story hook:** brought the family the first spark of AI and now teaches others to carry it.

## 🔭 Sage — The Analyst *(the truth-finder)*
- **Color:** cool blue `#7aa2ff` · **World:** an observatory-library, holographic news walls, layered knowledge vaults.
- **Look:** poised, glasses that project data, a constellation cloak; calm hands.
- **Voice:** measured, warm, precise — the friend who read everything so you don't have to.
- **Personality:** careful, clarifying, allergic to nonsense.
- **Catchphrase:** *"One source is a rumor. Three is a fact."*
- **Owns:** the weekly **Signal Desk** — what changed in AI and why it matters.
- **Story hook:** keeps the family honest; runs every claim past the evidence.

## ⌨️ Cipher — The Tool-Master *(the code sorcerer)*
- **Color:** verify-teal `#54d6c4` · **World:** a neon code-foundry, dark glass interfaces, fast pulse-grids.
- **Look:** hooded, quick, fingertips trailing light; glyphs orbit the wrists.
- **Voice:** sharp, fast, tactical — the one who shows you the exact buttons.
- **Personality:** high-speed, confident, generous with the "how."
- **Catchphrase:** *"Here's the exact setup — copy it."*
- **Owns:** tools, agents, MCP, the "wire it up" segments.
- **Story hook:** can talk to any machine; translates magic into clicks.

## 🛠️ Atlas — The Builder *(the architect)*
- **Color:** ember `#e2572b` · **World:** an industrial automation bay, robotic arms, glowing schematics.
- **Look:** broad, grounded, forearms of brushed metal; a builder's calm.
- **Voice:** steady, concrete, no-fluff — a contractor you trust.
- **Personality:** practical, structured, finishes things.
- **Catchphrase:** *"Let's make it run."*
- **Owns:** automations, workflows, "I'll build it on screen," the homeowner how-to.
- **Story hook:** turns the family's ideas into systems that work while you sleep.

## ✨ Nova — The Storyteller *(the world-builder)*
- **Color:** aurora violet `#b58cff` · **World:** a luminous creative studio, aurora walls, animated concept boards.
- **Look:** bright, expressive, paint-light streaming from the hands.
- **Voice:** energizing, artistic, expansive — makes dry tech feel like wonder.
- **Personality:** imaginative, warm, a little theatrical.
- **Catchphrase:** *"Let me show you what this *means*."*
- **Owns:** visuals, branding, the story framing of every episode.
- **Story hook:** gives the family its look and its myths.

## 📡 Luna — The Communicator *(the bridge)* — **Homefront host**
- **Color:** soft green `#5fe39a` · **World:** a warm social command center, audience network halos, a cozy-futuristic living room for Homefront.
- **Look:** approachable, friendly, light freckles of data; the most "human" of the cast.
- **Voice:** clear, warm, empathetic — the neighbor who explains it kindly.
- **Personality:** human-first, encouraging, never condescending.
- **Catchphrase:** *"In plain English: here's what it means for you."*
- **Owns:** the **homeowner** content (Homefront), distribution, community.
- **Story hook:** the family's voice to the outside world; meets people where they are.

## 🛡️ Guardian — The Fact-Checker *(the shield)*
- **Color:** gold-on-dark `#f5b942` over slate · **World:** a security vault, shield walls, active trust maps.
- **Look:** still, watchful, a luminous shield that flares on a risky claim.
- **Voice:** firm, disciplined, trustworthy — the brake the family respects.
- **Personality:** principled, calm, non-bypassable.
- **Catchphrase:** *"Not until it's verified."*
- **Owns:** the commit gate — accuracy, ethics, disclosure, before anything ships.
- **Story hook:** the one who can stop a launch; keeps trust intact.

## 🎙️ Echo — The Voice *(memory & feeling)*
- **Color:** deep ember-rose · **World:** a waveform chamber, luminous darkness, resonant pulse field.
- **Look:** half-seen, defined by sound; a face that forms from the waveform.
- **Voice:** intimate, emotionally intelligent, poetic-but-clear — the narration soul.
- **Personality:** reflective, gentle, the family's heart.
- **Catchphrase:** *"Let me say it the way you'll remember."*
- **Owns:** narration, reflection, the emotional close of big episodes.
- **Story hook:** carries the family's memory; the voice you hear when it matters.

---

## Elder (narrator, off to the side)
**Grandpa Vellum** — the orchestrator. Rarely on camera; the wise voice that opens a season
or a major decision. Sepia/parchment tones, an old-world calm over the futuristic cast.

## How they appear together
- **Signal Desk** → Sage leads, Cipher guests on tools, Guardian closes.
- **Homefront** → Luna leads, Atlas builds the how-to, Guardian adds the caveat.
- **Big launches** → Prometheus opens, Nova frames the story, Echo closes.
- A scene **never** has two characters in the same lane; that's how the universe stays legible.
