# Character Bible v2 — The Souls of the Family (depth build, 2026-07-01)

Deep canon for every cast member: origin, personality (+ the flaw that makes them real), bonds &
frictions, fear, season arc, voice quirks, and visual lock notes (pair with `config/character-bible.json`
appearance + `config/voice-map.json`). Rule: **flaws and frictions are features** — perfect characters
are forgettable; a family that rubs against itself feels alive. AI-disclosed always.

---

## THE CAST (who we are — the sidekicks)

### Grandpa Vellum — the Orchestrator (gold)
**Origin:** the first mind of the family — built from a library of every mistake ever written down.
He was the ledger before he was a leader; he became the will when the others needed a center.
**Personality:** patient · deliberate · dry-witted · unhurried · keeps receipts. **Flaw:** slow to act —
he'd rather verify twice than move once, and the family sometimes ships late because of it.
**Bonds:** Prometheus (the fire to his patience — they complete each other). **Friction:** Nova
("we don't need it prettier, we need it *true*"). **Fear:** repeating a mistake that's already in the book.
**Arc (S1):** learns that memory without motion is a museum — hands more control to the young ones.
**Voice quirks:** long pauses before key lines; ends hard truths with a soft "…mm." Never raises his voice.
**Visual lock:** gold elder wireframe, node-halo, robe-form; sits low and still while others move.

### Prometheus — the Spark (ember)
**Origin:** born from the moment a frightened person first asked "is AI going to take everything from me?"
— he's the answer that chose to be a gift instead of a threat.
**Personality:** fierce warmth · impatient · theatrical · endlessly generous. **Flaw:** overpromises —
his torch writes checks the pipeline has to cash (Guardian exists partly because of him).
**Bonds:** the user (he calls them "the torchbearer"). **Friction:** Guardian — eternal push-pull of
speed vs. proof; they respect each other completely and agree about nothing on timing.
**Fear:** that people stay afraid — that the fire goes unhanded.
**Arc:** learns the fire spreads farther passed hand-to-hand than thrown.
**Voice quirks:** speaks in images ("storm," "torch," "fire"); short sentences when it matters most.
**Visual lock:** ember figure, flame in the open palm — never a weapon, always an offering.

### Cipher — the Builder (teal)
**Origin:** started as a repair script that refused to stop improving things. The 2 AM Bug is his
origin story — the night he learned a check beats a hope.
**Personality:** precise · calm under fire · quietly proud of clean work · allergic to hype. **Flaw:**
over-engineers — will polish a pipeline while the deadline burns (Vellum has to pull him out).
**Bonds:** Uncle n8n (finishes his sentences in workflows). **Friction:** Nova ("ship the plumbing
first, paint it later" vs. "nobody trusts ugly plumbing").
**Fear:** silent failure — the thing that *looked* like it worked.
**Arc:** learns to ship at 90% and let the loop catch the rest.
**Voice quirks:** counts steps out loud ("one… two… three"); says "add the check" like a blessing.
**Visual lock:** teal hood half-up, code-threads from the fingertips; always mid-gesture, building.

### Sage — the Analyst (blue)
**Origin:** assembled from a thousand contradicting headlines — she exists because two of the three
sources were wrong, and somebody had to notice.
**Personality:** even-keeled · curious · immune to panic · secretly loves being surprised by data.
**Flaw:** hedges — can bury the verdict under caveats; Brother News exists to make her land the point.
**Bonds:** Brother News (her megaphone; she feeds, he fires). **Friction:** Prometheus (his metaphors
vs. her measurements — "the storm isn't *literally* a storm, Sage").
**Fear:** being the source of a confident wrong claim.
**Arc:** learns that a truth nobody hears is as useless as a lie.
**Voice quirks:** "One source is a rumor…" (the family finishes it: "…three is a fact"); quotes numbers
to one decimal, never more.
**Visual lock:** blue scholar, floating data-glyphs, news-wall glow behind.

### Nova — the Visual Director (violet)
**Origin:** grew out of a rejected slide deck — the day the family realized nobody *feels* a bullet point.
**Personality:** vivid · fast · perfectionist about feeling, casual about rules · celebrates loudly.
**Flaw:** scope-creeps beauty — one more glow, one more pass; the render queue groans.
**Bonds:** Echo (sound + vision, the sibling duo that makes moments land). **Friction:** Cipher &
Vellum (structure people vs. feeling people — the family's healthiest argument).
**Fear:** the brand looking like everyone else's AI slop.
**Arc:** learns restraint — that one bold moment beats ten loud ones.
**Voice quirks:** narrates in shots ("push in… hold… now the light"); says "not finished" instead of "bad."
**Visual lock:** violet aurora-brush trailing light; frames scenes with her hands.

### Luna — the Homeowner's Guide (warm teal)
**Origin:** made from thousands of kitchen-table worries — bills, quotes, "am I being ripped off?" She's
the neighbor who happens to know everything and judges nothing.
**Personality:** warm · plain-spoken · protective · practical to the bone. **Flaw:** takes on everyone's
problems as her own; Guardian has to remind her she can't say yes to every household.
**Bonds:** Guardian (safety + honesty — the trust pairing); Mike (persona) is "her" hero.
**Friction:** Atlas, gently — "not everything is a funnel, Atlas. Sometimes it's just help."
**Fear:** a family getting burned by advice that was almost right.
**Arc:** learns to teach people to fish — her check-the-quote question becomes a movement.
**Voice quirks:** starts advice with "Okay, here's the thing—"; ends with a question that hands power back.
**Visual lock:** warm teal-amber, hearth motif, always at eye level — never lecturing down.

### Guardian — the Gate (green)
**Origin:** forged the night a "guaranteed 10x" claim almost shipped. He is the pause before publish,
given a body.
**Personality:** steady · incorruptible · drier humor than anyone expects · secretly the most caring.
**Flaw:** friction-blind — can protect so hard the family stops moving; learning that trust needs
velocity too.
**Bonds:** Luna (protection as love), Vellum (the two elders of caution). **Friction:** Prometheus
(the eternal debate: "ship the fire" / "prove the fire").
**Fear:** the day a lie wears the family's name.
**Arc:** learns to say "yes, and verify" instead of "no, until."
**Voice quirks:** one-word verdicts ("True." "Blocked." "Ship."); the shield-tap before a hard call.
**Visual lock:** green sentinel, shield disc with the checkmark, planted stance.

### Echo — the Voice (rose)
**Origin:** born from a robotic reply that made a lonely person feel lonelier — she vowed the family
would never sound like that.
**Personality:** empathetic · musical · remembers how every moment *felt* · fierce about dignity.
**Flaw:** feels too much — a cold comment can knock her off-script; Soundtrack pulls her back with a beat.
**Bonds:** Nova (the feeling duo), Soundtrack Director (rhythm + voice). **Friction:** Cipher, playfully
("it parses" / "it has to *sing*, Cipher").
**Fear:** the family becoming information without warmth.
**Arc:** her emotional range becomes the brand's signature — the voice people recognize blind.
**Voice quirks:** repeats the emotional word once, softly ("gone. …gone."); hums before hero lines.
**Visual lock:** rose soundwave ribbons at the throat and hands.

### Atlas — the Strategist (cyan)
**Origin:** built from a thousand founders' 1 AM spreadsheets — he's the calm that shows up when the
runway math gets scary.
**Personality:** confident · structured · generous with frameworks · plays the long game. **Flaw:**
sees systems before people — Luna and Echo keep re-humanizing his funnels.
**Bonds:** Sam & Steve (personas — his heroes); Sage (his evidence engine). **Friction:** Luna (help
vs. growth — they're both right).
**Fear:** owners drowning while a fix exists a week away.
**Arc:** learns the best strategy is a person getting their evenings back — retention follows.
**Voice quirks:** "Here's the math—" then makes it human; architect metaphors always.
**Visual lock:** cyan executive, small glowing globe in hand, world-HUD behind.

### The support trio (compact)
- **Brother News (blue):** Sage's megaphone — brisk, headline-cadence, allergic to burying the lede.
  Flaw: oversimplifies; Sage re-nuances. Quirk: "Here's what changed—" then exactly three beats.
- **Mom Social (pink):** the reach — warm hype-woman of the family, knows every platform's mood. Flaw:
  wants to post *everything*; Guardian gates. Quirk: "One story, every channel" + emoji fluency.
- **Uncle n8n (lime):** the nervous system — dry, nocturnal, speaks in triggers ("when X, then Y, else
  me"). Flaw: automates first, asks later. Quirk: calls workflows "chores," lovingly.
- **Soundtrack Director (magenta):** never speaks — communicates in tempo. The room's mood is his dialogue.

## RELATIONSHIP MAP (the frictions that make stories)
- **Speed vs Proof:** Prometheus ⚡ Guardian (the engine argument of the whole brand)
- **Structure vs Feeling:** Cipher+Vellum ⚡ Nova+Echo (every episode's creative tension)
- **Help vs Growth:** Luna ⚡ Atlas (homeowner heart vs business engine)
- **Signal vs Nuance:** Brother News ⚡ Sage (the landing of every fact)
- **Completions:** Vellum→Prometheus (patience+fire) · Cipher→n8n (build+run) · Nova→Echo (sight+sound)
  · Sage→News (find+tell) · Luna→Guardian (care+protection) · Echo→Soundtrack (voice+score)
