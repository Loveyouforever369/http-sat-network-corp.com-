# Descript Alternatives — free replacements, researched & picked (2026-07-01)

**Why:** Descript ran out of AI credits (lesson #20). Fallback doctrine fired: research a free tool
that does the same job (script → voice + captions + stock B-roll + music → mp4). Live Tavily sweep,
June-2026 pricing verified across ngram.com, capcut.com, choppity.com, genesysgrowth.com.

## The verdict (fact that decides it)
Descript's **free** tier: 1 hr media/month, **720p, watermarked**. CapCut's **free** tier: **unlimited
editing, 1080p, watermark-free**, auto-captions, AI transcription, text-based editing. So for our use,
the free replacement isn't a compromise — **it's better than Descript's free tier.**

## The picks (in order)
| Tool | Free tier (verified 2026-06) | Why for us |
|---|---|---|
| **CapCut desktop** ⭐ | Unlimited editing, 1080p watermark-free, auto-captions, transcription, TTS, effects | The full Descript-free replacement; already in our stack docs; Win+Mac |
| **Clipchamp** | Unlimited 1080p, no watermark, TTS voiceover, auto-captions, stock | **Built into Windows** (user is on Windows) — zero-install fallback |
| **DaVinci Resolve (free)** | Full pro NLE; transcription/text-editing in Studio ($295 lifetime) | The pro finisher for the movie (already in EP-0310 pipeline) |
| **VEED.io** | Free tier, browser | Browser-only quick edits |

## The honest split (locked)
- **On the user's machine:** CapCut (or Clipchamp, zero-install) fully replaces Descript-free for the
  render step: paste the narration → TTS voice → auto-captions → stock/AI B-roll → 1080p mp4. Feeds
  straight into `automation/WORK-ORDERS.md` items.
- **In the sandbox (MCP):** there is **no free MCP-drivable video tool in this session** — Descript
  remains the only renderer I can drive from here, and it needs credits. So: sandbox renders = Descript
  (when credits exist); everything else = CapCut/Clipchamp locally via the work orders. No pretending otherwise.
