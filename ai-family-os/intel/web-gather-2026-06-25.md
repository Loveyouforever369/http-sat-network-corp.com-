## 2) "AI vs the $5k/mo agency" — feeds Atlas's flagship episode

- SMB **agency retainers: $5,000–$15,000/mo**. **AI marketing agents: ~$799–$1,999/mo**.
- Two tool classes: **passive** (you operate — ChatGPT, Jasper, Canva AI) vs **active agents** (run workflows autonomously).
- AI now handles content, scheduling, email flows, keyword research, reporting, SEO audits, and ad optimization — around the clock.
- **The honest take (Guardian-approved framing):** best setup for ~80% of SMBs is **hybrid** — AI execution + 5–10 hrs/mo of a senior human for strategy & QC. AI does **not** replace creative direction, relationships, or strategic pivots.

Sources: [Enrich Labs — replace your agency with AI](https://www.enrichlabs.ai/blog/how-to-replace-your-marketing-agency-with-ai) · [Enrich Labs — 35 best AI marketing tools 2026](https://www.enrichlabs.ai/blog/best-ai-marketing-tools-2026) · [searchlab.nl — honest answer for SMBs](https://searchlab.nl/en/guides/can-ai-replace-marketing-agency) · [gomega.ai — AI vs in-house](https://www.gomega.ai/blog/marketing-agency-alternative/)
