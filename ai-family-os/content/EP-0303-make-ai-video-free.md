## THE LONG CUT (YouTube, 8–10 min)

### COLD OPEN (0:00–0:12)
**[Nova]** "Everyone thinks a viral AI video comes from one secret tool. It doesn't. It comes from eight small steps — and almost all of them are free. I'm Nova. Let me show you our exact forge."

### STATION 1 — FIND (study what works)
**[Nova]** "Don't guess what to make. Pull the winners in your niche and read the *patterns* — the hook, the pacing, the structure."
**[Sage cameo]** "Free tools: NotebookLM to digest transcripts, a quick web search for what's trending. Study it. Then make *your* version — never copy."

### STATION 2 — SCRIPT (hook in 3 seconds)
**[Echo]** "Open with a question or a stake. One clear idea in the middle. A call to action at the end. Write it in your host's voice — character is what makes it yours."
**[On-screen]** Free: Claude / ChatGPT · Formula: Hook → Value → CTA

### STATION 3 — CAST (lock your characters)
**[Nova]** "Here's the #1 quality tell: do your characters look the *same* in every scene? Save one reference image per character and reuse it everywhere. Seedance and OpenArt do this free. Consistency is what reads as 'professional.'"

### STATION 4 — STILLS (design every frame first)
**[Nova]** "Generate your key images *before* you animate — it's cheaper and you get to actually art-direct. Hold your look. Ours is void-navy, gold, ember, teal. Cinematic, never clip-art."
**[On-screen]** Free: GPT Image · Leonardo · OpenArt

### STATION 5 — MOTION (bring stills to life)
**[Cipher]** "Now image-to-video, one shot at a time. Open-source **Wan** is free if you self-host. **Kling** nails physics. **Veo** syncs audio. Make short clips — you'll assemble them later."

### STATION 6 — VOICE (kill the robot sound)
**[Echo]** "Flat text-to-speech is the fastest way to scream 'amateur.' Use expressive, emotion-aware voices — a different one per character. Kokoro and IndexTTS-2 are free and good. This is the robotic-voice fix everyone asks about."

### STATION 7 — CUT (captions, music, rhythm)
**[Soundtrack Director cameo]** "Captions, because most people watch on mute. A score that matches the mood. Tight edits. We drive this from Descript — captions, transcript, publish, all in one."

### STATION 8 — SHIP (one long → many shorts)
**[Nova]** "Publish the long cut on YouTube. Then slice three to six vertical Shorts from the best moments for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. One forge run feeds every channel."

### CLOSE / CTA (the rule)
**[Nova]** "Here's the only rule that matters: AI video isn't dead — low-effort *slop* is. Every tool I showed you, a million people already have. We win by being the least sloppy channel in the room. Care is the craft."
**[On-screen]** Subscribe · Full free-tool list in the description · *We build in public.*

## SHORT #1 — "8 steps, mostly free" (0:40)
"Everyone wants the secret AI-video tool. There isn't one — there's a pipeline. Find what works → script with a 3-second hook → lock your characters → design your stills → animate with free tools like Wan and Kling → give it a real voice → caption and score it → ship one long video and cut 5 shorts from it. The tools are free. The care is the secret. Follow @TheAIFamily."

## SHORT #2 — "Why your AI video looks cheap" (0:35)
"Two reasons your AI video looks amateur: your character changes face every scene, and your voice is flat robotic TTS. Fix one: save a reference image and reuse it (Seedance, OpenArt — free). Fix two: use an expressive voice like Kokoro or IndexTTS-2. Do just those two and you jump a tier. — Nova"

## SHORT #3 — "Turn 1 video into 6" (0:30)
"Don't make six videos. Make one good long video, then cut six vertical shorts from the best 15-second moments. One script, one render, every platform fed. That's how faceless channels actually scale. Follow for the full free pipeline."
