The pipeline

Eight stations, one finished video.

Raw idea goes in the left, a finished, on-brand video comes out the right. The glow is work moving down the line — research to publish.

Station by station

The method, taught.

Each station has a job, a family member who owns it, and a free tool that does the work. This is the part you copy.

Find

Study what already works

Sage · the Analyst

Don't guess. Pull the winners in your niche and read the patterns — hooks, pacing, structure. Study, then make your own version. Never copy.

free: NotebookLM · Tavily
Script

Hook in 3 seconds

Echo · the Voice

Scene-by-scene: open with a question or a stakes line, deliver one clear idea, end on a call to action. Write it in the host's character.

free: Claude / ChatGPT
Cast

Lock your characters

Nova · Visual Director

The #1 quality tell: do your characters look the same in every scene? Save a reference per member and reuse it. Consistency reads as production value.

free: Seedance · OpenArt
Stills

Design every frame first

Nova · Visual Director

Generate the key images before animating — it's cheaper and you art-direct properly. Keep our look: void-navy, gold, ember, teal. Cinematic, never clip-art.

free: GPT Image · Leonardo · OpenArt
Motion

Bring stills to life

Cipher · the Builder

Image-to-video, one shot at a time. Open-source Wan is free to self-host; Kling nails physics; Veo syncs audio. Short clips, then assemble.

free tiers: Wan · Kling · Hailuo · Veo/Flow
Voice

Kill the robot sound

Echo · the Voice

The fastest "amateur" giveaway is flat TTS. Use expressive, emotion-aware voices — per character. See our Voice Lab for the rated shortlist.

free: Kokoro · IndexTTS-2 · Qwen3-TTS
Cut

Captions, music, rhythm

Soundtrack Director

Auto-captions (most watch muted), a score that matches the mood, tight cuts. This is where "fine" becomes "finished." We can drive this from Descript.

free: CapCut · Descript
Ship

One long → many shorts

Mom Social · Distribution

Publish the long cut on YouTube, then slice 3–6 vertical Shorts/Reels/TikToks from the best moments. One forge run feeds every channel.

free: YouTube · Zapier/n8n routing
The only rule that matters
"AI video isn't dead — low-effort AI slop is."

Every tool here is one a million people already have. The difference between a channel that grows and one that dies is care: a real script, characters that stay consistent, a voice with feeling, a look that's yours. We don't win by having secret tools. We win by being the least sloppy channel in the room.
