The 10-minute setup

Three steps, that's it.

Don't download ten apps. Do these three things and your household runs lighter by tomorrow morning.

STEP 1

Pick one "household brain"

Choose a single assistant — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or a done-for-you tool like Lindy — and make it yours. Tell it, in two sentences, who's in your household and what you want help with.

one assistant · 2-sentence intro
STEP 2

Connect only two things — observe-only

Connect just your calendar and your email, and keep it in observe-only: it suggests, you approve. The pros who set these up for a living all say the same thing — never turn on automatic actions in week one.

calendar + email · it suggests, you approve
STEP 3

Give it three small jobs

"Summarize today's important emails." "What's on our calendar this week?" "Draft a reply to the school / the landlord / the doctor." That's 80% of household admin — handled before coffee.

summarize · schedule · draft
Guardian's note: stress observe-only first — never auto-send or auto-pay in week one, and don't connect banking in a beginner setup. Many "assistants" are chatbots in agent costumes; test before you trust. Relax one rule a week as it earns it.
