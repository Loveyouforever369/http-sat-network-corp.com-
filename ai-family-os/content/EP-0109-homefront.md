## COLD OPEN (0:00–0:14)
**[Luna]** "Most home projects blow the budget — about **78% go over**, and two-thirds of homeowners take on debt to finish. The worst part is the quote you can't make sense of. AI fixes exactly that. Let me show you."

## SEGMENT 1 — Paste the quote, ask three questions
**[Luna]** "Take any quote — roof, HVAC, solar, a remodel — paste it into an AI and ask: 'Is this fair? What's missing? What should I ask?' It instantly catches the omissions contractors sometimes leave out — like hauling the old cabinets, the dump fee, the cleanup — so you're not hit with a surprise charge at the end."
**[On-screen]** "Paste quote → 'fair? missing? what to ask?'"

## SEGMENT 2 — Compare bids apples-to-apples
**[Luna]** "Got two or three bids that look nothing alike? Free tools like BidCompareAI line them up side by side, standardize them, and flag missing items and unrealistic allowances — so you compare the *real* numbers, not the formatting."
**[Atlas]** "That's the move — never compare bids by gut. Make them line up first."
**[On-screen]** "Compare bids → standardized, red flags surfaced"

## SEGMENT 3 — The red flags to never ignore
**[Luna]** "And let AI help you spot the classic scam signs the Better Business Bureau warns about: demands cash up front, showed up at your door uninvited, 'we can start today,' or 'we've got leftover materials from a job nearby.' Real, busy contractors rarely do any of those."
**[On-screen]** "Red flags: cash up front · uninvited · 'start today' · 'leftover materials'"
