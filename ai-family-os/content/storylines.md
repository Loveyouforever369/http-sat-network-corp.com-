# The AI Family — Storylines (render-ready shorts)

Eight character-driven shorts. Each is a real *situation* that teaches one thing — fun,
cinematic, 30–45s, faceless-or-avatar ready. Format per beat: **[V]** visual · **[VO]**
line · **[M]** music. Every short ends on the same CTA: *Follow the AI Family · link below.*

> Guardian rule: any tool/claim stays generic + honest; AI-host/voice disclosed; verify specifics.

---

## EP-0201 · "The First Spark" — Prometheus (origin)
**Logline:** a person drowning in AI hype meets the one who reframes it.
- [V] A tired founder doomscrolls 50 "AI will replace you" headlines. [M] anxious hum.
- [VO Prometheus] "They told you to be afraid. I'm here to hand you the fire instead."
- [V] Gold light gathers in his hand; the headlines reorganize into a simple plan. [M] orchestra lifts.
- [VO] "AI isn't the storm. It's the tool that gets *you* through it."
- **Takeaway:** stop fearing AI; start directing it. (The family's reason to exist.)

## EP-0202 · "The 2 AM Bug" — Cipher
**Logline:** a small-business automation breaks at 2 AM; Cipher fixes it live.
- [V] A dark room, one red error blinking; orders not going out. [M] glitch pulse.
- [VO Cipher] "Your automation didn't fail. It just had no guardrail."
- [V] Teal code-light threads through the wires; the error turns green. [M] tension → release.
- [VO] "An agent without a check is a faster mistake. Add the check. Sleep again."
- **Takeaway:** automate with guardrails, not hope.

## EP-0203 · "The Headline That Lied" — Sage
**Logline:** a terrifying AI headline turns out to be mostly air.
- [V] "AI ENDS [your job] BY FRIDAY" flashes on a phone. [M] ominous.
- [VO Sage] "One source is a rumor. Let's find three."
- [V] Holographic news wall; two of three sources quietly contradict it. [M] calm piano.
- [VO] "The truth was calmer than the headline. It usually is."
- **Takeaway:** verify before you panic (or repost).

## EP-0204 · "The One-Person Marketing Team" — Atlas
**Logline:** a solo founder doing everything; Atlas builds the make→post loop.
- [V] One person juggling camera, captions, calendar — dropping all of it. [M] frantic.
- [VO Atlas] "You're not lazy. You're un-automated."
- [V] An assembly bay lights up: idea → script → post, moving on rails. [M] heroic low-end.
- [VO] "Automate the boring 90%. Keep the 10% only you can do."
- **Takeaway:** systems beat hustle.

## EP-0205 · "Making It Make Sense" — Nova
**Logline:** impenetrable jargon becomes something a grandparent gets.
- [V] A wall of acronyms (LLM, RAG, MCP) looming over a confused face. [M] muddled.
- [VO Nova] "Big words are where understanding goes to hide."
- [V] Aurora light melts the wall into a simple picture-story. [M] soaring future-pop.
- [VO] "If a kid can't follow it, it's not finished."
- **Takeaway:** clarity is the real superpower.

## EP-0206 · "The Worried Homeowner" — Luna  *(ties to Homefront EP-0109)*
**Logline:** a homeowner afraid of being overcharged; Luna hands them the AI quote-check.
- [V] A kitchen table, a scary contractor quote, a worried face. [M] tense, warm.
- [VO Luna] "Before you sign anything — let's ask AI one question."
- [V] Phone overlay: "Is this fair? What's missing?" → padded line items glow red. [M] reassuring.
- [VO] "You don't need to be an expert. You need to ask the right question."
- **Takeaway:** AI makes you the most prepared person at the table.

## EP-0207 · "The Launch That Almost Shipped" — Guardian
**Logline:** a too-good claim is one click from publishing; Guardian stops it.
- [V] Finger over a glowing PUBLISH button; the post promises "guaranteed 10x." [M] rising.
- [VO Guardian] "Stop. Is that true — or just the first version?"
- [V] A shield flares; the claim rewrites itself into something honest. [M] firm resolve.
- [VO] "Speed without a check is a faster mistake. Trust is the brand."
- **Takeaway:** the gate exists for a reason.

## EP-0208 · "The Voice That Remembered" — Echo  *(emotional close)*
**Logline:** why the family speaks like humans, not machines.
- [V] A lonely late-night kitchen; a phone reads a cold robotic reply. [M] hollow.
- [VO Echo] "Information isn't enough. People remember how you made them feel."
- [V] The waveform warms into a human voice; the room softens. [M] intimate, tender.
- [VO] "We'll always say it the way you'll remember."
- **Takeaway:** AI should feel human — that's the whole point.
