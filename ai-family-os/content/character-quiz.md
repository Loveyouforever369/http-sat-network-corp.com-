# "Which AI Sidekick Do You Need?" — The Character Quiz (lead magnet)

The front door of the funnel. 12 questions → maps every taker to one of the 6 personas
(`config/customer-personas.json`) → a tailored result page with 3 quick wins + the matching story +
CTA. Build in **Tally / Typeform / Google Forms** (free), or as a page on the site. Owner: Atlas + Mom Social.

## Scoring
Each answer maps to a persona letter: **A=Sarah · B=Mike · C=Sam · D=Chris · E=Pat · F=Steve.**
Most-picked letter = their sidekick match. Ties → the later question wins (Q9–Q12 weigh intent heavier).

---

## THE 12 QUESTIONS

1. **What best describes your work right now?**
   A) I'm a coach/consultant/creator doing everything myself · B) I run a local shop/restaurant/service with a small team · C) My business is growing and my team is stretched · D) I run an agency/creative studio · E) I'm in a licensed/compliance-heavy profession · F) I'm building a startup

2. **What eats most of your week?**
   A) Content + admin + follow-ups · B) Scheduling, inventory, reviews · C) Lead handling + onboarding chaos · D) Client revisions + proposals · E) Documents, intake, billing · F) Literally everything at once

3. **What's your biggest fear about AI?**
   A) It'll erase my voice — I'll sound like everyone else · B) It's for big chains, not my team · C) It'll break my sales process · D) It'll cheapen my craft · E) Hallucinations = liability; confidentiality · F) Burning cash on the wrong tools

4. **A lead or customer reaches out. What usually happens?**
   A) I reply personally… eventually · B) Whoever's free answers · C) Sales follow-up is inconsistent · D) I'm too deep in client work to respond fast · E) Intake is a pile of forms · F) I answer at 1am myself

5. **What would 10 extra hours a week mean to you?**
   A) More clients + actual weekends · B) Time to work ON the business, not in it · C) Founder-level strategy again · D) Real creative work again · E) High-value advisory work · F) Shipping faster

6. **Your content/marketing situation?**
   A) Good when I have time — consistency is killing me · B) Barely any — no time, small budget · C) We post, but it's not systematic · D) Great for clients, invisible for ourselves · E) Minimal — referrals carry us · F) Spiky — launch bursts, then silence

7. **Your data/tools situation?**
   A) A few apps, nothing talks to each other · B) POS + spreadsheets · C) A CRM nobody updates · D) Project tools + too many tabs · E) Secure systems, strict rules · F) New stack every month

8. **What would you automate FIRST if it just worked?**
   A) Content repurposing + follow-ups · B) Reviews + scheduling + inventory alerts · C) Lead qualification + onboarding · D) Research, proposals, revisions · E) Document review + intake · F) Research + investor updates

9. **How technical are you?**
   A) Not very — it has to be simple · B) I can use apps, not build them · C) My team can handle setup · D) Comfortable with creative tools · E) Cautious — vetted tools only · F) I'll try anything tonight

10. **What proof would convince you?**
    A) Someone like me getting hours back without losing their voice · B) A local business cutting waste/costs · C) Pipeline numbers going up · D) An agency doing 2–3x output, same quality · E) A compliant, private setup that lawyers/accountants trust · F) Speed: idea → shipped

11. **Budget reality for solving this?**
    A) Start small ($27–$497), grow with results · B) Modest monthly if ROI is obvious · C) Will invest ($3K+) in something that scales · D) Mid-range for leverage · E) Will pay for *secure* done-right · F) Scrappy now, bigger post-raise

12. **Six months from now, success looks like…**
    A) Working 20 fewer hours, earning more, sounding MORE like me · B) A calmer shop, better ratings, less waste · C) A team that runs without me in every meeting · D) An agency known for craft, not grind · E) A practice with capacity for premium work · F) Traction, runway, and a system that scales
