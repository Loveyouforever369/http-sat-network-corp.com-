## THE LONG CUT

### COLD OPEN (0:00–0:15)
**[Grandpa Vellum]** "Most people use AI by winging it — a tool here, a prompt there, hope for the best. We don't. We run a locked-in process that gets sharper every week. Here's the whole thing, so you can steal it."

### STEP 1 — Sense (find what's true + what's working)
**[Grandpa Vellum]** "We start by looking, not guessing. Live research — what changed, what's working — before we make anything. One source is a rumor; three is a fact."
**[On-screen]** "1. Sense — research first (free: Tavily, NotebookLM)"

### STEP 2 — Script (the story + the hook)
**[Vellum]** "Then we write it for a real person: a hook in the first three seconds, one clear idea, a reason to act. In a character's voice, so it's ours."
**[On-screen]** "2. Script — hook → value → CTA"

### STEP 3 — Make (the free tool stack)
**[Nova]** "Now we build. For video: draft free on **Meta AI**, finalize on **Google Flow** with consistent characters and audio. **Seedance** for character shots. Open tools like **Wan** when we want to own it."
**[Cipher]** "For voice we use our Voice Lab — Kokoro, IndexTTS-2 — so it has real feeling, not robot-flat."
**[On-screen]** "3. Make — Meta AI → Flow · Seedance · our Voice Lab"

### STEP 4 — Check (the honesty gate)
**[Vellum]** "Then Guardian checks it. Every stat sourced. AI disclosed. No promise we can't keep. We enforce it in code, not just good intentions."
**[On-screen]** "4. Check — Guardian gate (sources · disclosure · truth)"

### STEP 5 — Remember (why we get sharper)
**[Vellum]** "Here's the part most skip. Whatever worked, we write down to do again. Whatever broke, we write down to never repeat. A system that remembers is a system that compounds."
**[On-screen]** "5. Remember — lock what works + what doesn't"

### STEP 6 — Ship (one becomes many)
**[Nova]** "One long video becomes a handful of shorts for every platform. One run, fed everywhere — YouTube, TikTok, Reels, Facebook."
**[On-screen]** "6. Ship — one long → many shorts"

### CLOSE / CTA
**[Grandpa Vellum]** "Sense, script, make, check, remember, ship. The tools are mostly free — the *process* is the edge. That's why a small family runs like a studio. Follow along, and we'll teach you every step."
**[On-screen]** Subscribe · *We build in public, every week.*
