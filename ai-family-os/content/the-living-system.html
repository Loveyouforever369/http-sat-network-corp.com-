How it grows

The loop that makes it alive.

A tool runs once and forgets. An organism repeats a loop and gets better each time. This is ours — every cycle leaves the family a little sharper.

Sense

Sage reads the live web for what changed and what people need.

Think

Vellum routes it and the council weighs options before acting.

Make

Cipher & Nova build the page, the script, the visual, the voice.

Check

Guardian gates it — sources real, claims true, AI disclosed.

Remember

Every lesson is written to the Journal so it's never re-learned.

Teach

What we learned becomes a Behind-the-Build episode and a course.

Guardian's note: "Coming alive" is a metaphor for an honest engineering pattern — specialized roles, a routing brain, a quality gate, and a memory of mistakes. There's no magic here; there's a loop that compounds. We say exactly where each capability lives and we never claim an action happened that didn't.
