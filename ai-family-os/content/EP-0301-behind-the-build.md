## MISTAKE 1 — We promised to publish before checking we could
**[Cipher]** "We said 'we'll post this' — then found the tool wasn't even connected, and the network blocked it. Classic."
**[Guardian]** "Rule: verify the tool's connected and the destination's reachable *before* you promise. Saying 'I can't yet' beats faking 'done.'"
**[On-screen]** "Fix: verify the path before you promise it."

## MISTAKE 2 — We retried a broken button instead of stepping back
**[Cipher]** "A render tool kept failing mid-call. We hit it again. Same fail. Wasted cycles."
**[On-screen]** "Fix: don't hammer a failing tool — stage the job, fire when it's healthy."

## MISTAKE 3 — We 'corrected' a fact that was already right
**[Cipher]** "We told the boss 'that model's not out yet.' It was. We corrected from memory, not a source."
**[Guardian]** "A correction needs a source as much as a claim does. Check before you overrule."
**[On-screen]** "Fix: verify before you correct."

## MISTAKE 4 — A silent failure we didn't catch
**[Cipher]** "A folder change made our save command quietly match nothing — we *thought* we shipped, but nothing moved."
**[On-screen]** "Fix: confirm the result. Print the receipt. Don't assume success."

## MISTAKE 5 — We planned when we should've produced
**[Cipher]** "Told to 'just go,' we sometimes asked one more question instead of shipping."
**[On-screen]** "Fix: when it's go-time, produce. Save questions for real forks."
