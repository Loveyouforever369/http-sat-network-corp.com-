## SEGMENT 1 — Pick one "household brain" (the reasoning layer)
**[Luna]** "Don't download ten apps. Pick *one* assistant — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or a do-it-for-you tool like Lindy — and make it your household brain. Tell it, in a sentence, who you are and what you want help with."

## SEGMENT 2 — Connect only two things, in *observe-only* mode
**[Luna]** "Connect just two: your calendar and your email. And keep it in *observe-only* — it suggests, you approve. The pros who set these up for a living all say the same thing: never turn on automatic actions in week one."
**[On-screen]** "Step 2 · calendar + email · it suggests, you approve"

## SEGMENT 3 — Give it three small household jobs
**[Luna]** "Now give it three jobs: 'Summarize today's important emails.' 'What's on our calendar this week?' 'Draft a reply to the school / the landlord / the doctor.' That's 80% of household admin, handled before coffee."
