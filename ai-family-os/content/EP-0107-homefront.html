The 3 fixes

Where AI quietly saves you money.

1

Stop overpaying for energy

AI energy assistants read your usage and tell you — in plain English — when you're wasting money and whether a cheaper plan exists. You forward one bill; it does the math you'd never sit down to do.

forward a bill → "find waste + a cheaper plan"
2

Kill the silent subscriptions

AI money assistants scan your statements, surface every recurring charge, and even draft the cancel-or-negotiate message for you. Most households find something they're paying for and not using.

scan statements → cancel what you forgot
3

Never overpay a contractor

Before you accept any home quote — roof, HVAC, solar — paste it into an AI and ask, "Is this fair, and what should I ask?" It catches padded line items and hands you the exact questions a pro would ask. That one habit can save thousands.

paste a quote → "is this fair? what should I ask?"

Always get it in writing and verify the contractor's license. AI helps you ask better questions — it doesn't replace doing the check.
