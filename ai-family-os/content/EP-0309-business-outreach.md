## THE LONG CUT

### COLD OPEN (0:00–0:15)
**[Atlas]** "If you run a business, your most expensive problem isn't marketing — it's that the work only you can do keeps getting buried under work anyone (or any AI) could do. Here's exactly what an AI crew takes off your plate, what it costs, and why it's worth it."

### THE PROBLEM (0:15–1:00)
**[Atlas]** "You're the owner, the marketer, the support desk, and the scheduler. Every hour on busywork is an hour not spent on the thing that actually grows the business. Agencies fix this for $5,000–$15,000 a month. Most owners can't — or shouldn't — start there."

### WHAT WE ACTUALLY DEPLOY (1:00–3:30)
**[Cipher]** "We don't hand you a chatbot. We wire specific workflows:"
- **Lead response** — every inquiry gets a fast, personal first reply, day or night. No lead goes cold.
- **Customer answers** — the same 20 questions, drafted in your voice, in seconds.
- **Content engine** — one topic → a long video + shorts + posts, on a schedule (this video was made that way).
- **Scheduling + intake** — bookings, reminders, forms, without the phone tag.
- **First-pass research** — vendors, competitors, quotes — summarized and sourced.
**[Mom Social]** "And it shows up everywhere — one long video becomes six shorts across every channel."

### WHY IT WORKS — THE HONEST MATH (3:30–4:45)
**[Atlas]** "An AI crew runs about $799–$1,999 a month versus $5k–$15k for an agency. But the real win is the model: AI does the execution, you keep strategy and the final say. It's a hybrid — not a robot replacing you, and not you doing it all alone. We start with the ONE workflow that moves your numbers, prove it, then add the next."

### THE GUARDRAILS (4:45–5:30)
**[Atlas]** "Two promises. One: a human stays on the gate — nothing sends, posts, or pays without approval until you trust it. Two: we never sell you a fantasy. No 'guaranteed 10x.' Just the boring busywork gone, so you get your hours back."

### CLOSE / CTA
**[Atlas]** "Book a free call. We'll find your highest-leverage workflow, show you exactly how it'd run, and set it up with you. Educate, show, then do it for you."
