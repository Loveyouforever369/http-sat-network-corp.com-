## THE LONG CUT

### COLD OPEN (0:00–0:12)
**[Guardian]** "We've made real mistakes building an AI brand — and we wrote down every one so we never repeat it. Here are the dos and don'ts, turned into rules that keep *you* safe and sharp."

### DO ✅ — verify before you trust
**[Guardian]** "AI can be confidently wrong. Always check facts, numbers, and dates against a real source before you act on them. A correction needs a source too."
**[On-screen]** "DO: verify before you rely."

### DON'T ⛔ — paste private or sensitive data
**[Guardian]** "Don't drop passwords, client data, or anything you wouldn't email a stranger into a public AI tool. Keep secrets out; use private/self-hosted tools for sensitive work."
**[On-screen]** "DON'T: paste secrets."

### DO ✅ — keep a human on the gate
**[Guardian]** "Let AI suggest; you approve. Never turn on automatic actions — sending, paying, posting — until you trust it. The human-in-the-loop is the whole safety net."
**[On-screen]** "DO: human approves before it acts."

### DON'T ⛔ — believe the hype (or the doom)
**[Guardian]** "Free tiers, 'unlimited,' the 'best model' — it all changes monthly. Don't pick tools by hype; pick by what works for your job today, and re-verify."
**[On-screen]** "DON'T: trust hype — re-verify."

### DO ✅ — disclose when it's AI
**[Guardian]** "If a voice or a host is AI, say so. We disclose it on every page — because trust is the brand. Honesty compounds."
**[On-screen]** "DO: disclose AI. Trust compounds."

### DON'T ⛔ — let it run without a receipt
**[Guardian]** "Don't assume it worked. Confirm the result — the email sent, the file saved, the post went live with an ID. No receipt, not done."
**[On-screen]** "DON'T: assume — confirm."

### CLOSE / CTA
**[Guardian]** "Six rules: verify, protect your data, keep a human on the gate, ignore the hype, disclose the AI, and confirm the result. Follow them and AI becomes a superpower you can trust. We live by these — now they're yours."
**[On-screen]** Subscribe · *The full list lives in our build journal.*
